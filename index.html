Before you apply

The Founding Team Charter

A good-faith agreement, not a legal contract. It sets clear expectations so we build with honesty on both sides.

Our mission

AEGIS is a technology company building AI and software systems for the next generation — technology that impacts generations and advances the Kingdom of Christ.

Who is a founding team member

Someone who joins AEGIS at its earliest stage, contributes meaningfully, and shares its mission. This is a voluntary, collaborative role with no salary at this time — not employment.

What we expect from each member

  1. Contribute the hours you committed to on joining.
  2. Communicate regularly and honestly.
  3. Treat AEGIS work, plans, and code as confidential.
  4. Act in the best interest of AEGIS and its mission.
  5. Give at least 7 days' notice before leaving.

What members can expect from AEGIS

  1. A clear role and clear tasks.
  2. Honest communication about progress and challenges.
  3. Public credit for your contributions.
  4. Portfolio-worthy work and real experience.
  5. A seat at the table when AEGIS formalizes its legal structure and equity framework.

On equity and rewards

AEGIS has no legal entity, shares, or valuation yet, so no equity amount can be promised today. When AEGIS incorporates, we'll design a fair, transparent equity framework together — founding members who contributed meaningfully will receive a stake in it. Details will be documented and signed at that time. No verbal equity promises: if it isn't in writing, it doesn't exist.

On intellectual property

All work created for AEGIS — code, designs, documents, ideas — belongs to AEGIS and its team, not to any individual.

On leaving

Members may leave with at least 7 days' notice. AEGIS may release a member if expectations are consistently unmet. Either way, we part with respect.
